Forager

Privacy Policy

Forager helps you discover dishes from cuisines around the world, plan meals for your household, and turn your cart straight into a shopping-ready grocery list — all without an account.

Updated on August 9, 2026

1 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Forager ("the App") interacts with when you use it, how that information is stored, and the choices available to you. Forager is designed to work fully on your device — no account, sign-up, or login is required to browse dishes, save favorites, plan meals, or generate grocery lists.

2 Information We Collect

Forager does not require you to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, or other identifying information to operate the App.

The following information is created and stored locally on your device as you use the App:

  • Dishes you mark as favorites
  • Items and meal plans (number of meals, adults, and children) in your current cart
  • Grocery lists you generate, including current and historical lists
  • Search and cuisine filter preferences

This data is used solely to make the App function as intended and is not transmitted to Forager's developer or any third party.

No account required No ads No third-party trackers No data sold

3 How Local Data Is Used

Favorites, cart contents, and grocery lists exist to give you a continuous meal-planning experience between sessions. This information stays on your device and is used only to display your saved dishes, carts, and lists back to you within the App.

4 Data Storage & Security

Because App data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's own security features (such as screen lock or disk encryption). If you uninstall the App or clear its storage, this data is permanently deleted. Forager does not maintain servers that store your personal meal-planning data.

5 Third-Party Services

Forager does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics SDKs, or social login providers. If this changes in a future update, this Policy will be updated in advance of the change taking effect.

6 Children's Privacy

Forager is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Meal and household details (such as number of children per meal) entered for planning purposes are stored locally and are not linked to any individual's identity.

7 Your Choices

  • You can delete individual favorites, cart items, or historical grocery lists at any time within the App.
  • You can clear your entire cart from the cart page at any time.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's features. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
